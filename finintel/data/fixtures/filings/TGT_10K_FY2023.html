PART I

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our results depend on discretionary consumer demand, which may be affected by inflation and higher interest rates.

Inventory shrink, including theft and organized retail crime, increased significantly and reduced our gross margin; we closed several stores in part due to theft and safety concerns.

We face intense competition from multi-category retailers, warehouse clubs and digital marketplaces.

A failure to manage inventory levels could require markdowns.

We rely on information systems and could be subject to a cybersecurity incident.
