PART I

Item 2. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Comparable sales decreased 1.9 percent in the second quarter, reflecting a decrease in comparable store traffic partially offset by growth in digital comparable sales. Discretionary categories, particularly home and apparel, remained soft, while food and essentials and beauty were relatively stable.

Gross margin rate decreased primarily due to higher markdowns and merchandising costs, partially offset by lower supply chain costs. Tariff-related cost increases are expected to pressure margins in the second half of the year.

PART II

Item 1A. Risk Factors

There have been no material changes to the risk factors disclosed in our most recent Annual Report, except as follows: the announced transition of our Chief Executive Officer may create uncertainty among team members, vendors and investors, and the imposition of additional tariffs on goods imported from several sourcing countries has increased the risk that our product costs will rise faster than we can offset.
