PART I

Item 1A. Risk Factors

We are highly dependent on the financial performance of our U.S. and Canadian operations and on membership renewal rates.

We face strong competition from other retailers and warehouse club operators.

Tariffs and changes in trade policy could increase merchandise costs; our limited-assortment model allows us to adjust sourcing quickly.

We could be subject to a significant cybersecurity incident.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Net sales increased 5.0 percent and comparable sales increased 5.3 percent excluding gasoline and foreign exchange. Membership fee income increased reflecting new member sign-ups, a renewal rate of approximately 90 percent in the U.S. and Canada, and the fee increase. Traffic increased across regions.
