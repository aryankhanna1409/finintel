PART I

Item 1A. Risk Factors

We face strong competition from other retailers, wholesale club operators and omni-channel retailers, which could materially adversely affect our financial performance.

Changes in trade policy, including tariffs, could increase the cost of merchandise; our scale and sourcing flexibility partially mitigate this risk.

Our e-commerce, advertising and membership businesses depend on continued investment in technology and automation; failure to execute could affect our growth.

We could be subject to a significant cybersecurity incident.

Our international operations subject us to foreign currency and geopolitical risks.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Consolidated net sales increased 5.1 percent, driven by comparable sales growth and strong e-commerce performance, with transaction counts and unit volumes increasing. Share gains were led by upper-income households. Operating income grew faster than sales as higher-margin businesses such as advertising and membership scaled.
