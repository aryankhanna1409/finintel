PART I

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is sensitive to the financial condition of our core customer, who has been financially constrained by inflation.

Inventory shrink and store standards issues have affected our results and our reputation with customers.

We have substantial operating lease obligations and indebtedness, which could limit our flexibility.

Tariffs could increase the cost of consumables and discretionary goods.

We face intense competition from mass merchants, grocers and digital retailers.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Net sales increased 5.0 percent; same-store sales increased 1.4 percent driven by higher average ticket. Gross margin declined due to higher shrink and markdowns. Our back-to-basics plan focuses on store standards, labor investment and inventory reduction.
