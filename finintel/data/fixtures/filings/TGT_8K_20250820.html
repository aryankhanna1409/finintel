FORM 8-K: Target Corporation (SYNTHETIC FIXTURE excerpt)

Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers

The Board of Directors approved a chief executive officer succession plan under which the current Chief Operating Officer will become Chief Executive Officer effective at the start of the next fiscal year and the current Chief Executive Officer will transition to the role of Executive Chair.

Item 2.02. Results of Operations and Financial Condition

The Company reported second quarter comparable sales of negative 1.9 percent and reaffirmed its full-year adjusted EPS guidance range of approximately $7.00 to $9.00.

Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure

Management outlined an enterprise acceleration office focused on speed, technology and merchandising authority, and reiterated its commitment to the dividend and to strong investment-grade ratings.
