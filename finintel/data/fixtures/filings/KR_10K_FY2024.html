PART I

Item 1A. Risk Factors

The termination of the proposed merger with Albertsons and related litigation may divert management attention and result in costs.

We face intense competition in the grocery industry, including from mass merchants and warehouse clubs.

Our pharmacy business is subject to reimbursement pressure and regulation.

Labor relations and the cost of union contracts could increase operating expenses.

Food inflation and changes in consumer behavior may affect our results.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Identical sales without fuel increased 2.0 percent, led by pharmacy and digital. Following the termination of the merger agreement, we executed a $7.5 billion accelerated share repurchase program.
